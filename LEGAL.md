## 2. Privacy Policy

### 2.1. Who we are
NFT Prompt Builder (w3ir.io), contact **w3ir@pm.me**.

### 2.2. Data we process
- **Wallet address** (public) — identifier for credits and history.
- **On-chain balance reads** (native balance for anti-Sybil) via RPC nodes.
- **Generation data** — your prompts, generated images, metadata, history — stored **on our server,
  isolated per wallet**.
- **Payment data** — transaction ID, amount (via Helio; **crypto only**, we never see bank/card data).
- **Session cookie** `w3ir_session` (HMAC) — for sign-in.
- **Server logs** (IP, basic) — for security and rate-limiting; aggregate Cloudflare analytics.
- **We do NOT collect** email, name or other traditional personal data; there is no account beyond
  the wallet.

### 2.3. Why
To provide the Service, account for credits, prevent abuse/Sybil, and improve the product.

### 2.4. Who we share with
- **AI providers** (OpenAI, Anthropic, Stability, Replicate) — your prompts/images for generation.
- **Pinata/IPFS** — files if you publish to IPFS (content becomes **public and immutable**).
- **Helio** — to process payments. **Cloudflare** — for delivery/protection.
- Each party has its own policy. **We do not sell your data.**

### 2.5. Retention
History is kept until you request deletion or while the wallet is active; logs are rotated.
**On-chain and payment transactions are immutable on the blockchain** — outside our control.

### 2.6. Security
HMAC sessions, secrets kept out of code, database backups. No system is perfectly secure.

### 2.7. Your rights
You may request **deletion of your wallet's stored data** — email w3ir@pm.me. On-chain data cannot
be deleted.

### 2.8. Cookies
Only an **essential** session cookie; Cloudflare may set technical/security cookies.

### 2.9. Children
The Service is not intended for anyone under 18.

### 2.10. Changes

## 3. Refund Policy

### 3.1. Nature of payments
Payment is by **cryptocurrency (USDC on Base)**; such transfers are **technically irreversible**.
Credits are digital, consumable units.

### 3.2. Non-refundable
- **Consumed credits** (spent on generations) are non-refundable.
- **Welcome, referral and granted** credits — promotional, with no cash value.

### 3.3. Considered case-by-case
Refunds of **unused** credits are considered individually, within **14 days**, for a technical issue:
double charge, payment confirmed but credits not delivered, etc. Contact us with your **wallet address
+ transaction hash**.

### 3.4. Failed generations
If a generation fails, credits are **automatically refunded** to your in-app balance — no action
needed.

### 3.5. How to request
Email **w3ir@pm.me** with your wallet address and transaction hash. We respond within
a **reasonable time** (typically a few business days). Chargebacks do not apply (crypto payment).
